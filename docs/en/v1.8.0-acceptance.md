## Boundaries and subsequent verification

1. This trial-license instance has no real primary/standby topology. Formal licensing, replication, primary/standby failover, terminal failure outcomes and production workloads remain **unverified**. Reconnecting to one instance or hot-reloading a certificate does not establish HA.
2. This release does not expand the SQL subset and does not grant PostgreSQL B2 column-level binding or B5 cross-request transaction capabilities to Vastbase. Other G100 builds, protocol modes, CPU/system and manufacturer drivers have not been verified.
3. Dameng DM8 server-side identity-verifiable TLS and formal active-standby switching continue to be unverified; the capability boundaries of KingbaseES and HighGo are still subject to their respective official acceptances.
