## production support boundary

DM8 Pack3, KingbaseES V9 PG mode and Vastbase G100 Build 9 only perform bounded regression on specified instances and synthetic data. Complex JOIN, TLS, HA, long-term load and real customer environments have not yet completed full production acceptance, and the database support level will not be upgraded accordingly.
