## unproven capabilities

1. Dameng DM8 currently uses Go to drive `github.com/godoes/gorm-dameng` v0.7.2's `security.NewTLSFromTCP` and hardcode `InsecureSkipVerify: true`. AgentSQL continues to reject Dameng explicit TLS options; acceptance of the correct CA/wrong CA/hostname and rotating real database is not complete and cannot claim to be able to verify the server's identity. [ upstream implementation of ](https://github.com/godoes/gorm-dameng/blob/main/dm8/security/zzi.go), [Dameng communication encryption instructions ](https://eco.dameng.com/document/dm/zh-cn/pm/communication-encryption.html).
2. The formal active-standby topology, failover final state, and multi-agent capacity conclusions of KingbaseES and Dameng have not yet been completed. Single-instance disconnection recovery cannot replace active-standby switching.

The above two items are not included in this version of the support statement, and will be accepted separately after obtaining the driver and active/standby topology conditions; they are not used as published alternative evidence of verified KingbaseES single-instance TLS capabilities.
