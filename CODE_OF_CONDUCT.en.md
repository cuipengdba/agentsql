# Contributor Convention

**Language:** [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) | [Simplified Chinese](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## our commitment

As community members, contributors and leaders, we are committed to ensuring that community participants are free from harassment regardless of age, body shape, visible or invisible handicaps, ethnicity, sexuality, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, social and economic status, national origin, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual orientation or orientation.

We are committed to acting and interacting in ways that help build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, healthy community.

## Our Code

Examples of behaviors that help create a positive environment for our community include, but are not limited to:

- Show compassion and kindness to others
- Respect different opinions, opinions and feelings
- Offer and accept constructive opinions generously
- Take responsibility and apologize to those affected by our mistakes and learn from them
- Focus on the common aspirations of the community rather than personal gains and losses

Examples of misconduct include:

- Use of sexualized language or images, and any form of sexual attention or teasing
- Ridiculing, insulting or derogatory comments, as well as personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publish another person's private information, such as physical address or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other misconduct that can be reasonably determined to be a violation of professional ethics

## Responsibilities and authority

Community leaders have a responsibility to interpret and implement our endorsed standards of conduct and to take appropriate and fair corrective action against any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Community leaders have the authority and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, submissions, code, Wiki edits, Issues, or other contributions that do not comply with this Code of Conduct, and to provide reasons for moderation action when appropriate.

## Scope of application

This Code of Conduct applies in all community settings and also when individuals formally represent the community in public spaces.

Examples of representing the community include using an official email address, posting via official social media accounts, or serving as a designated representative at online or offline events.

## Processing policy

Community leaders will follow the following community policies to clarify how they will respond to violations of this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

Community Impact: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior that is considered unethical or unpopular in the community.

Action: A non-public written warning from a community leader that clearly states the nature of the violation and explains how the conduct was inappropriate. Community leaders may demand a public apology.

### 2. Warning

Community Impact: A single or series of violations.

Action: Issue a warning and explain the consequences of continuing this behavior. No interaction with relevant persons, including proactive interactions with Code of Conduct enforcers, is allowed during designated times. This includes avoiding interactions in community venues and external channels such as social media. Violation of these terms may result in temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

Community Impact: Serious violations of Community Guidelines, including ongoing inappropriate behavior.

Comments: Any form of interaction or public communication with the community is temporarily prohibited during the specified period. During this period, there will be no public or private interactions with relevant individuals, including unsolicited interactions with Code of Conduct enforcers. Violation of these terms may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

Community Impact: A pattern of behavior that demonstrates violations of community standards, including continued inappropriate behavior, harassment of individuals, or attacks or disparages of a category of individuals.

Action: Any form of public interaction within the community is permanently prohibited.
